## Two people, eleven callers

On September 29, 2026, Acuity Health’s voice agent was on the phone with 11 Abita Eye Group patients at the same moment. Each caller had a separate conversation with the agent.

Before Acuity Health, Abita Eye Group’s phones were covered by a two-person outsourced call center. Each person can answer one call at a time, and calls don’t arrive evenly through the day. Abita Eye Group came to Acuity Health because patient calls were being dropped.

September 29 was not a one-off. Between August 9 and October 27, three or more patients were on the phone with the agent at the same moment on 56 of 80 days. A two-person team could not have taken all of those calls at once.

The agent also takes calls at night and on weekends. More than 1,000 calls and 73 booked appointments came in outside 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern, Monday through Friday. Across the 80 days, patients spent about 717 hours on the phone with the agent, with an average call of about 2.6 minutes.

Abita Eye Group retired its outsourced call center once the agent took over its patient calls.

## Books in AdvancedMD with Abita Eye Group’s own rules

Acuity Health is an [AdvancedMD marketplace partner](https://www.advancedmd.com/integrations/marketplace/acuity-health/). The agent schedules directly in Abita Eye Group’s AdvancedMD schedule by provider, location, appointment type, insurance and specialty, and books the appointment while the patient is on the phone.

Acuity Health worked with Abita Eye Group’s team to build the practice’s own scheduling rules into the agent:

- Which doctors take which insurance plans
- Which doctors see pediatric patients
- When and where a provider can be double-booked

Take a parent who calls at 9 p.m. about a child’s eyes. The agent applies Abita Eye Group’s rules and offers a doctor who sees children and takes the family’s plan, in an opening AdvancedMD shows as available.

Each of the 1,472 appointments the agent booked came back from AdvancedMD with an appointment ID, so the practice can check every booking in its own system.
